Course Description

Welcome to ActiveVibe 9, your go-to space for all things Physical Education! This course empowers students to live healthy, active lifestyles through structured movement, teamwork, and fitness-based learning.

Learning Objectives:

  • Improve physical fitness, motor skills, and endurance.
  • Understand rules, strategies, and values in sports and games.
  • Practice safety and teamwork in every activity.
  • Embrace healthy habits for lifelong wellness.

Learning Goals:

  • Maintain a fitness routine.
  • Demonstrate sportsmanship and leadership.
  • Apply movement concepts in real-life situations.
  • Value physical activity as a foundation for a healthy life.

Class Schedule:

Tuesday & Thursday – 9:00 AM to 10:30 AM
Friday – Skill Drills and Group Games, 2:00 PM to 3:00 PM

Class Calendar Highlights:

  • Week 1–2: Physical Fitness and Movement Skills
  • Week 3–4: Team Sports (Basketball, Volleyball)
  • Week 5–6: Individual/Dual Sports (Badminton, Table Tennis)
  • Week 7–8: Dance Fitness and Aerobics
  • Week 9–10: Nutrition, Health & Wellness Seminar

Class Rules and Regulations

  • Be punctual and wear the complete PE uniform.
  • Always bring a water bottle and towel.
  • Respect everyone’s ability and effort.
  • No rough play or unsafe behavior.
  • Submit performance logs and reflections on time.
